f. Maintain visual contact with other aircraft

a. Press AP/A/R disconnect (control stick)
b. Maintain visual contact with the tanker until clear
c. If visual is lost attempt to establish radar contact with tanker
d. Turn on rotating beacon lights when clear

GPWS/TAWS Terrain Warning
PF
1. Throttles — MAX/TOGA
2. Disengage AP/ATS
3. Roll wings level rotate to 15° pitch (disregard F/D)
4. Maintain config unless flaps beyond half → retract to half
5. If terrain remains threat increase pitch to ALS/stick shaker/CAWS onset
6. Maintain pitch until clear of terrain
PM
1. Verify maximum thrust
2. Verify speedbrakes retracted
3. Call out any omissions to required actions
4. Monitor vertical speed and radar altimeter
5. Call out any trend toward terrain contact
Clear of Terrain
1. Monitor radar altimeter for sustained/increasing terrain separation
2. Monitor TAWS display for increasing terrain separation
3. Slowly decrease pitch attitude and accelerate
